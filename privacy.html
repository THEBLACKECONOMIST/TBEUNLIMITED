BlackCalc Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

BlackCalc ("the app," "we," "our") is an all-inclusive financial and economics calculator. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

No Data Collection

BlackCalc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or calculation data. Specifically:

  • All calculations are performed entirely on your device.
  • Numbers, inputs, and results you enter are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs).
  • No data from BlackCalc is sold, shared, or used for advertising purposes.

Permissions

BlackCalc does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other device data beyond what is strictly required for the app to run.

Third-Party Services

BlackCalc does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services. Because the app is distributed through Google Play, Google's own Play Store policies and services (such as delivery and installation) apply independently of this policy — see Google's Privacy Policy for details on those services.

Children's Privacy

BlackCalc does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If BlackCalc's data practices ever change — for example, if a future update adds optional cloud sync or analytics — this policy will be updated first, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
